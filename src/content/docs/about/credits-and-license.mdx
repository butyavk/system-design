The original is a single, community-maintained guide to designing large-scale systems and preparing for system design interviews, with sample solutions, object-oriented design exercises and Anki flashcards. Its map of topics, its choice of case studies and its reading lists are the foundation of this site. Thank you to Donne Martin and to everyone who contributed to it.

## What this adaptation changed

CC BY 4.0 requires an adaptation to say that changes were made. In this site, the original has been:

- **Restructured** from one long README into 13 sections of short pages with navigation and search.
- **Rewritten** in new words. Explanations, examples and diagrams were written afresh rather than copied.
- **Updated** for 2026: outdated claims corrected and newer practice added where it matters for design decisions (managed cloud services, HTTP/3, Kafka-style logs, distributed SQL, Valkey and Redis, edge compute, SLOs and others).
- **Expanded** with new pages and sections that the original did not cover in depth.
- **Translated into Russian**, as a natural technical text rather than a literal translation.
- **Given interactive tools**, such as calculators for estimates. The original's Anki decks are replaced by on-site [flashcards](/reference/flashcards/) with spaced repetition, plus Anki-importable decks in English and Russian generated from the same cards.
- **Re-sourced.** Dead links were replaced, archived or removed; further-reading lists were re-checked.

Because every page was rewritten, errors on this site are ours, not the original author's.
