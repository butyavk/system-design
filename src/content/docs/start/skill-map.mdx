Every topic of this guide on one map, in six stages from orientation to real-world systems. Each topic shows whether you have learned it, opened it or skipped it, and selecting it draws what to read first and what it leads to. The map follows your reading: pages you open are marked, and you mark a topic as learned at the end of its page.

<SkillMap />

## How to read the map

- **Stages** run top to bottom in the order the [study plan](/start/study-plan/) uses. Each stage ends with what you should be able to do once it is done.
- **Arrows** appear when you select a topic: solid teal lines come from the topics to read first, dashed amber lines go to the topics it unlocks.
- **Ready** means every topic it needs is learned, so the page will make sense now. **Skipped** marks a topic that something you have already learned builds on: the gaps worth closing before an interview.
- **Next up** suggests up to three ready topics from your current stage, in study-plan order. Pick a plan above the map to dim the topics your timeline leaves out.
- Prerequisites are deliberately few: only what a page really relies on. You can read in any order; the map only tells you where the ground is firm.
