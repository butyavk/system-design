Every topic of this guide on one map, in six stages from orientation to real-world systems. The overview tree at the top shows how the topics build on each other; below it, the same topics are listed by stage. Each topic shows whether you have learned it, opened it or skipped it, and selecting it draws what to read first and what it leads to. The map follows your reading: pages you open are marked, and you mark a topic as learned at the end of its page.

## How to read the map

- **The overview tree** reads left to right: each topic sits to the right of the topics it builds on, and the lines between them are prerequisites. **Full screen** opens the tree over the whole window; Esc closes it.
- **Stages** in the list under the tree run top to bottom in the order the [study plan](/start/study-plan/) uses. Each stage ends with what you should be able to do once it is done.
- **Lines** light up when you select a topic: solid teal lines come from the topics to read first, dashed amber lines go to the topics it unlocks.
- **The topic card** opens when you select a topic. On the tree it docks at the right of the map (under it on a narrow screen). It shows the topic's status and reading time, what to read first and what it leads to, and **Path here**: the topics on the way that you have not learned yet, how long they take to read carefully, and roughly how many days learning them takes in each plan.
- **Ready** means every topic it needs is learned, so the page will make sense now. **Skipped** marks a topic that something you have already learned builds on: the gaps worth closing before an interview.
- **Next up** suggests up to three ready topics from your current stage or the next one, in study-plan order. Pick a plan above the map: the number next to it is how many topics it covers, and topics outside it fold into small dots. The long plan covers every topic.
- Prerequisites are deliberately few: only what a page really relies on. You can read in any order; the map only tells you where the ground is firm.

## Your progress

- Pages you open are marked as opened automatically. Mark a topic as learned at the end of its page, in its card on this map, or by ticking it in the [study plan](/start/study-plan/): all three set the same mark.
- Progress lives only in this browser. There is no account and nothing is sent anywhere, so another browser or device starts empty, and clearing the site's data erases it.
- To move it, use **Download progress** under the map and **Load progress** on the other device. The file also carries your [flashcard](/reference/flashcards/) progress, the chosen plan and the map look.
- **Reset progress** clears the map and the study plan. Flashcards keep their own progress and their own reset.
- **Dark fantasy** at the top right of the map switches it to a skill-tree look; the topics and your progress stay the same.
